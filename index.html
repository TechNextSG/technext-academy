Questions

What principals ask us first.

Does it cost the school anything?

The seminar and bootcamp sessions are free for partner schools. Nothing is charged to the school or students unless it is written into the MOA and agreed before the day. The official Odoo exam is optional, and its fee is set and charged by Odoo.

What does the computer lab need?

Computers with an up-to-date browser and a stable internet connection. Odoo and the AI tools run online, so nothing needs to be installed. One computer per student works best; pairs are fine.

Who runs the sessions?

Trainers from TechNext's Odoo and AI team. TechNext is headquartered in Singapore, has a team in the Philippines and has delivered 142 ERP projects for companies.

Is the TechNext certificate accredited?

No. It is a company certificate that shows what the student completed, with a QR code anyone can use to verify it. It is not a TESDA, DepEd or CHED credential.

How many students can join?

A seminar takes 100 to 500 students in the gym or AVR. A bootcamp batch is 30 to 45 students, so a 40-seat lab runs one batch a day.

Does the OJT shortlist guarantee a placement?

No. Gold-level students can be referred to companies we work with for OJT. Acceptance is always up to the host company.
