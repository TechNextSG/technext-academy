EXAM BY ODOO S.A.

Odoo Certification prep

For Gold students and teachers. We coach the functional skills the official Odoo exam covers: sales, inventory, accounting and website.

How it runs

Start big, then go hands-on.

A seminar reaches the whole grade level. The bootcamp is for students who want to go further.

LEVEL I

Seminar

90 minutes · 100–500 students

Talk and live demo in the gym or AVR. Earns the Bronze badge.

LEVEL II

Bootcamp

1 day · 30–45 per batch

Hands-on in the computer lab, one track. Earns the Silver badge.

LEVEL III

Bootcamp Plus

2 days · team project

Build and present a small project. Gold badge, certification prep and an OJT shortlist.

For schools

Four steps to a bootcamp day.

  1. Book a 20-minute visitWe meet the principal or SHS coordinator and agree on tracks and dates.
  2. Sign a simple MOAOne page covering dates, venue, safety and data privacy.
  3. We promote it with youPosters, a QR registration form and a short Nexi video for your page.
  4. Run the day, share the resultsCertificates, photos (with consent) and a short report for the school.

What the school provides

  • A venue: gym or AVR for seminars, computer lab for bootcamps
  • A teacher or coordinator on site
  • Parent consent through the school's usual process
Student data stays minimal. We collect name, school, grade, strand and an email for the certificate, following the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173). We never send sales messages to students.
